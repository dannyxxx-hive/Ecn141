Formulae & Calculator

Key formulae

APC = Consumption ÷ Disposable Income
APS = Saving ÷ Disposable Income (= 1 − APC)
MPC = ΔConsumption ÷ ΔDisposable Income
MPS = ΔSaving ÷ ΔDisposable Income (= 1 − MPC)
Disposable Income = Total Income + Transfers − Taxes
GDP/GNP per capita = Total Output ÷ Population
Growth rate = [(GDPc − GDPp) ÷ GDPp] × 100
Terms of Trade = (Export Price Index ÷ Import Price Index) × 100
Aggregate Expenditure (open economy) = C + I + G + NX
Net Exports (NX) = Exports − Imports
